Introducing Centri ›

Today’s reporting is rebuilt by hand. The same weekly report, from the same three spreadsheets. Two people report the same number differently, and neither one is wrong. A number gets questioned in a meeting, and by the time it’s re-derived, the decision has already been made without it.

Centri is a command center for your numbers. Ask a business question in plain language and get an answer with its definition and source rows attached, so you stop rebuilding the report and start trusting what’s in it.
